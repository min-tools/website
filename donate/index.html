Donate

A direct way to support the apps, if you want to.

Min Tools is a late-night project. The apps are free to try, and a Pro purchase on the Mac App Store is the usual way to support them. If you would rather send something directly, these wallets are mine.

A donation is a gift, not a purchase. It does not unlock Pro, and nothing is tracked or expected in return. Send only on the network that matches the address.

These addresses are public, so anything sent to them is public too. For a larger amount, or if you would rather not use a public address, email crypto@min.tools and I will send you a fresh one.
