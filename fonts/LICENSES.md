  Instrument Serif Project Authors. https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif

Instrument Sans comes unchanged from the Twenty Twenty-Four WordPress theme,
which bundles it under the same license. Instrument Serif Italic comes from
`ofl/instrumentserif` in the Google Fonts repository, subset to Latin and
converted to WOFF2 with `pyftsubset`; the family declares no Reserved Font
Name.
