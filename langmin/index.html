Files, text, and conversations

Recordings become text, on your Mac

On macOS 26 or later, Apple transcribes supported languages on device for free. Drop an M4A, MP3, or WAV file, review the transcript, then use it in any mode.

Files read on device

Read PDF, DOCX, and TXT files locally, with on-device text recognition for images and scanned PDFs. Source documents stay on your Mac; extracted text follows your model-sharing permissions.

Fixes text where it already is

Select text in Mail, Pages, or Notes and use Proofread or Rewrite from Services to replace it in place. No Accessibility permission or simulated keystrokes are needed.

Follow up on anything

A proofread, a translation, a dictionary entry: every result is the start of a thread, and you can switch models halfway through it.

Your keys, your bill, or no bill

Apple Intelligence costs nothing and stays local. Cloud models run on your own account, straight to the provider. With Pro, a model on your own machine works too, through LM Studio or Ollama.

Warns before secrets leave

Secret Protection can warn before sending text that appears to contain passwords, tokens, or API keys to a remote provider. It is on by default.

Removes invisible text watermarks

Optional cleanup removes unwanted invisible characters used as text watermarks on your Mac. It preserves code blocks and characters needed for emoji and writing systems.

Several languages in one run

Translate into Serbian, Spanish, and Japanese at once, keep recent targets one click away, and let Langmin notice when the text is already in the target language. Serbian works in both scripts.

Built for learners

Recordings in, text out

Drop a recording. Keep the words.

Meeting audio, voice memos, lectures, interviews: drag an M4A, MP3, or WAV file into the input and the transcript lands as editable text, ready for any mode.

  • Transcribed on your Mac, free

    Apple speech recognition runs on device on macOS 26 or later. Choose a supported language or use your Mac's language. macOS may download a speech model after asking you. Recordings stay on your Mac, and Apple Intelligence is not required.

  • Or OpenAI with your key

    Pro can send a recording to GPT-Transcribe on your own OpenAI account: it detects the language and accepts files up to 25 MB. Langmin asks for separate audio-sharing permission before uploading.

  • Then do something with it

    The transcript is plain text in the input. Review it, then summarize the meeting, translate the interview, or proofread the dictated draft. You can choose different providers for transcription and writing.

  • Documents and images, the same way

    PDF, Word, and text files are read on your Mac; screenshots and scanned PDFs go through on-device OCR. Source documents are not uploaded. Extracted text follows the same model-sharing permissions as typed text.

Follow-ups

Ask a follow-up. Keep the thread.

Every result has a conversation under it. Ask for an example, a shorter version, another language, or a different tone, and the answer lands right below the original.

  • Request changes in plain words

    Each follow-up includes the original request, result, and recent conversation. Ask for changes such as “shorter” or “now in Serbian.” Return sends, Shift-Return adds a line, Escape keeps your draft.

  • Switch models mid-thread

    The picker under the field can use any model you have enabled, and every reply says which one wrote it.

  • Saved, narrated, printed, together

    Save to the Library and the whole thread comes along; new replies update the saved item. Narration reads the follow-ups too, ⌘P prints the conversation, and any reply can be edited in place.

Private by design

No Langmin accounts or servers.

Your text goes only where you send it, straight from your Mac to the provider you chose. Langmin's developer never sees it.

  • Straight to the provider, nowhere else

    Your text travels from your Mac to the AI you chose and back. Langmin operates no servers that receive your text. Remote providers handle it under their own policies.

  • Nothing leaves without your say

    The first time a provider would see your text, Langmin asks. Allow one request, remember permission, or cancel. You can reset permissions in Settings. Secret Protection can also warn about text that looks like a password or API key.

  • Your keys stay in your Keychain

    API keys live in the macOS Keychain, go only to their own provider, and can be removed any time.

  • No usage tracking

    No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no background clipboard reading. App data stays in its sandboxed container, with API keys stored separately in Keychain. iCloud Library sync stays off until you enable it.

Send text to OpenAI?

Langmin will send text for requests you make with OpenAI directly from your Mac using your API key. The provider may process or retain it under its privacy policy and terms. Langmin's developer does not receive the text.

The details

Small things, done carefully.

Clipboard etiquette

Langmin reads the clipboard only when you invoke an action. If you copy something else while a proofread is still running, it asks before replacing it.

Webpage reading

Paste a link into Explain or Summarize and Langmin fetches the page on your Mac, excerpts long pages, and keeps images only when you ask for a detailed answer.

Translate knows when to stop

If the whole text is already in a target language, Translate says so and leaves your clipboard alone instead of rewriting it.

Menu bar mode

Close every window and Langmin keeps running in the menu bar for shortcuts and Services; ⌘Q quits it. The Library opens in the main window or in its own, and Langmin remembers which you prefer.

Humanize

Humanize asks the model for more natural phrasing while preserving meaning. It does not promise to evade AI detectors.

Sandboxed

Langmin runs in the App Sandbox and uses macOS Services and the clipboard without Accessibility permission or simulated copy-and-paste keystrokes.

Requirements

An Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. Apple Intelligence needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on. Apple transcription also needs macOS 26 and a supported language. Cloud providers need your own API key.

Source available

The app's source is published on GitHub under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 with added permissions, so you can read exactly what leaves your Mac.
