Help Keymin grow

Keymin is made by Ilia Ross. Contributions are welcome.

Ways to contribute

  • Report a bug or suggest an improvement. Tell us what happened and what you expected, and which daemon you run. Remove private commands and paths from any configuration you attach.
  • Add a translation, or clarify a confusing instruction. Keymin starts in English.
  • Contribute code or review a change. Please follow the repository's contribution terms and keep changes focused.
  • Share Keymin with someone who uses skhd.

A Keymin Pro purchase on the App Store supports development.

Thank you for using Keymin and helping make it better.
