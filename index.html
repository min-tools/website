Pastemin

Your clipboard, ready when you need it.

Keep a searchable history of the text and images you copy. Press a shortcut in any app, type a word, and press Return to put the item back on the clipboard. Items marked concealed or temporary are skipped. You choose how long to keep the history, which stays on your Mac.

No tracking

No Min Tools accounts, analytics, or servers.

These apps keep their local data on your Mac and send no usage data to Min Tools. Network requests depend on the features you use: AI providers, websites, network checks, optional iCloud sync, and Apple purchases.

  • Kept in the sandbox

    App data stays in each app's own container. Langmin stores API keys separately in Keychain and can sync your Library through iCloud if you enable it.

  • You choose the features that connect

    Langmin connects to the providers and websites you use. Netmin contacts the systems needed for each check. Pastemin never uploads clipboard history.

  • Plain-language policies

    Each has its own privacy policy. This site generates its privacy pages from those policies and checks that the text matches.

  • Purchases through Apple

    Trials are counted on your Mac. Pro is bought through the App Store, and Apple never shares your payment details with us.

How they are built

The same rules, every time.

Native Swift

AppKit and SwiftUI on Apple silicon, following your Mac's appearance, contrast, accent, and reduced-motion settings.

Sandboxed

They run in the App Sandbox with Hardened Runtime and need no administrator access. Pastemin requests Accessibility access only for optional automatic paste.

Keyboard first

Use keyboard shortcuts for common actions. Langmin and Pastemin open from other apps; Netmin lets you find tools and run checks from the keyboard.

Localized

Each is available in more than twenty-five languages and follows your Mac's language settings. Translations are welcome as contributions.

Free to start

Each starts with 30 days of full access, without starting a subscription. A free tier remains afterward. Pro is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with Family Sharing where enabled.

Requirements

Langmin, Pastemin, and Netmin currently require an Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. Each product page lists its current requirements.

Nothing extra to install

No Homebrew or developer runtimes are needed. They include their own helpers; some Apple features may download on-device models.
