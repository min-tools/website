The trial and the free app

The 30-day trial begins after you accept its notice at first launch and includes full access to your clipboard history. No subscription starts and nothing is charged. After the trial, Pastemin keeps monitoring the clipboard, and the panel shows and searches the five newest items. Older items remain on your Mac and become visible again when Pro access is restored. Nothing is uploaded, moved, or deleted by the limit.

Pastemin Pro

Pastemin Pro restores access to the complete history. It is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase in two forms:

  • Yearly subscription. Billed to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically each year at the price shown at purchase unless you cancel it in your App Store settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Lifetime unlock. A one-time purchase that keeps Pro for the life of the app on the App Store.

Both can be shared with your family through Family Sharing where it is enabled. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency before you buy. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple Media Services Terms; we cannot issue refunds ourselves.
