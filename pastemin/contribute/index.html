Help Pastemin grow

Pastemin is made by Ilia Ross. Contributions are welcome.

Ways to contribute

  • Report a bug or suggest an improvement. Tell us what happened and what you expected. Leave out private clipboard content.
  • Improve one of the thirty-one translations or clarify a confusing instruction.
  • Contribute code or review a change. Please follow the repository's contribution terms and keep changes focused.
  • Share Pastemin with someone who could use it.

A Pastemin Pro purchase on the App Store supports development.

Thank you for using Pastemin and helping make it better.
