Help Netmin grow

Netmin is made by Ilia Ross. Contributions are welcome.

Ways to contribute

  • Report a bug or suggest an improvement. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and which tool you used. Remove private addresses, hostnames, and diagnostic output.
  • Improve one of the twenty-seven translations or clarify a confusing instruction.
  • Contribute code, a tool definition, or a review. Please follow the repository's contribution terms and keep changes focused.
  • Share Netmin with someone who could use it.

A Netmin Pro purchase on the App Store supports development.

Thank you for using Netmin and helping make it better.
